# Rewind - timing notes

Generated by `build_timing.py` / `write_notes.py`. All times in seconds from the start of `Rewind (4).mp3` (decoded length 232.400 s via ffmpeg/librosa; the container reports 232.44). mm:ss.mmm in tables.

## Method

- Vocal separation: UVR MDX-Net `Kim_Vocal_2` ONNX (numpy re-implementation, CPU, ~2 min) -> `stems/vocals.wav`, `stems/no_vocals.wav` (demucs/HF/pytorch hosts are blocked by the proxy; GitHub-hosted UVR weights work). Stems are gitignored.
- Beats/downbeats: madmom RNNDownBeatProcessor + DBN (4/4, 110-140 BPM) on the full mix, de-quantised by local linear fit. Cross-checked with librosa (agrees within ~25 ms, constant offset) and a comb-filter tempo fit.
- Words: Cloudflare Workers AI `@cf/openai/whisper-large-v3-turbo` on the vocal stem (word timestamps), sequence-aligned to the lyric sheet, then edges snapped to vocal-stem activity (-45 dBFS, 10 ms). Tape-stop tails, inhales and a few phrase starts were hand-verified on 10 ms energy / spectral-flatness traces.
- Envelopes: `envelopes.json`, 30 fps (frame i = i/30 s), keys rms/low/mid/high/vocal/inst/onset, each 0..1.
