Official character design turnaround sheet for an original anime music video heroine named DOT. Modern TV-anime key-art style: crisp confident black lineart, flat two-tone cel shading with one hard shadow tone, clean shapes, no gradients, no painterly texture, bright even studio lighting, plain very light warm-gray background. Layout: four full-body views in a row (front, three-quarter, side profile, back) standing in a relaxed neutral pose at identical scale, plus a row of three head close-ups: singing with mouth open wide on an "ah" vowel, soft hopeful smile, determined gaze looking up.
DOT is a 20-year-old East Asian young woman, slim athletic dancer build, long straight glossy jet-black hair to mid-back with blunt bangs and a hidden pale sky-blue under-layer that shows when the hair moves, large dark-brown eyes with a small pale-blue ring highlight. She wears chunky white over-ear studio headphones with orange ear cups resting around her neck, an oversized cropped white technical bomber jacket with a bright orange reflective stripe across the chest and two round embroidered patches (a tiny pale blue dot on black; the text "1420 MHz"), a black ribbed high-neck crop top, loose dark navy wide-leg cargo pants with orange utility straps, and chunky white sneakers. In the full-body views she holds a small handheld Yagi antenna (thin aluminum rods on a boom, orange grip) at her side.
Consistent face, hair, outfit and proportions across every view. Character sheet, model sheet, reference sheet.
